Should I rent or buy a copier?
Renting suits most businesses: no large upfront cost, maintenance and toner are bundled into one predictable monthly fee, and you can upgrade as you grow. Buying can make sense if you print steadily and prefer to own the asset. We'll compare both options for you.
What's included in a rental plan?
Delivery, installation, network setup, preventive maintenance, repairs, technician visits and genuine toner. Paper is the only thing you supply.
Which areas do you cover?
We serve the whole Klang Valley from our office in Petaling Jaya, including Kuala Lumpur, PJ, Subang Jaya, Shah Alam, Puchong, Cyberjaya and Putrajaya.
How fast can you fix a breakdown?
Being local means our technicians can reach you quickly. Response times are written into your service agreement, so you know exactly what to expect.
Can you take over servicing machines we already own?
Yes. We can inspect your existing copiers and printers and offer a maintenance contract, or suggest a cost-effective replacement if a machine is past its best.
How long is a rental contract?
We offer flexible terms to suit your business. Tell us your plans and we'll recommend a term that keeps your monthly cost low.
